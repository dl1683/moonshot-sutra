## I218: Lessons From PCCP-H

### Core Question

What did PCCP-H actually teach about intelligence, absorption, and the next direction?

### Analysis

#### Steelman

PCCP-H killed a seductive mistake cleanly:

```text
If a system finds invariants, relations, decompositions, and proof obligations,
then it is discovering intelligence structure.
```

The absorption ladder supplied the missing distinction:

```text
If the field universe, intervention grammar, relation grammar, verifier, and
decomposition testbed are supplied by humans, then the system may only be
enumerating inside the frame.
```

That is the real signal. Intelligence is not search inside the right space. Intelligence is finding the space where search becomes cheap.

What the ladder says:

- B1 invariance was not intelligence because exhaustive single-field checking with equal information found the same clause.
- B2 metamorphic relation mining was not intelligence because exhaustive relation mining over supplied `T` and `Phi` found the same clauses.
- B3 decomposition discovery was not intelligence because exhaustive interaction tests recovered the same boundary.
- B3.5 synthesis value was real, but it belonged to using a boundary, not to a non-absorbed boundary-discovery mechanism.

The hard part is frame formation:

```text
What is a thing?
What can change?
What must remain invariant?
What is the right intervention?
What is a valid failure?
Which measurement stays aligned with the target function?
Which decomposition makes repair cheap?
```

Directions more resistant to PCCP-H absorption must target one of these:

1. **Frame creation under hidden-family transfer.** The system produces a transformation, verifier, decomposition, or intervention language not already in the supplied grammar.
2. **Frame transfer to a weaker learner.** The output is a compact lesson, program, verifier, or curriculum that makes a cheap learner acquire the frame.
3. **Function-aligned compression.** The artifact ignores nuisance entropy while preserving counterfactual behavior.
4. **Self-generated tests against proxy failure.** The system discovers when its own metric no longer measures the function.
5. **Open-ended intervention choice under strict query budget.** The system chooses what evidence is worth buying, against fair active-learning baselines.

#### Hostile Attack

A hostile reviewer can say:

```text
You only learned that toy finite mechanisms are absorbed by toy exhaustive
baselines. That is not a theory of intelligence.
```

This attack has force. "Too expensive to exhaust" is not the same as "intelligent." A heuristic can still be conceptually absorbed if it only searches the same human-provided grammar.

The next direction must therefore measure a sharper object:

```text
Did the system create or transmit a useful frame, or did it merely explore an
available one?
```

### Verdict

```text
PCCP-H taught that the next moonshot must target frame creation or frame
transfer, not supplied-frame enumeration.
```

Use absorption testing as the immune system. Do not make absorption testing the moonshot.

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

```text
You spent twenty batches learning that a for-loop can beat a fancy name.
Calling that a theory of intelligence is just rebranding failure.
```

Answer:

```text
Correct against PCCP-H as a discovery engine. Incorrect if the negative result
is used as a filter: the next target is the thing the for-loop did not do,
namely discovering or transmitting the frame that makes the problem small.
```

## I219: Direction Candidates

### Core Question

What plausible next moonshot directions serve the manifesto, have a simple story, fit the hardware, and resist trivial absorption?

### Analysis

#### Steelman

Cast wider than "which model architecture?" Ask what missing object would make intelligence cheap. Scores are 1-10, higher is better.

| ID | Candidate | Viral Narrative | Disruption | Narrative | Feasibility | Absorption Resistance |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | FrameSeed / Intelligence Vaccines | "Teach a tiny AI the right five lessons instead of training a giant on the whole internet." | 9 | 10 | 7 | 7 |
| B | Self-Discovered Transformation Grammars | "An AI that figures out what changes matter." | 8 | 8 | 7 | 6 |
| C | Pocket Proof Compiler | "Rent the giant AI once, keep the cheap verified program forever." | 8 | 9 | 8 | 5 |
| D | Energy-Native Answer Economy | "AI gets cheap when it stops talking and starts judging." | 7 | 8 | 7 | 4 |
| E | Semantic Algebra | "A calculator for meaning." | 10 | 9 | 4 | 5 |
| F | Failure-Patch Immune System | "AI that gets vaccinated after every mistake." | 8 | 9 | 8 | 5 |
| G | Proxy-Function Divergence Science | "Why AI can ace the test and fail the job." | 6 | 7 | 8 | 6 |

#### A: FrameSeed / Intelligence Vaccines

Core claim:

```text
A cheap learner can acquire a new capability from a tiny frame-bearing lesson
instead of massive data or blind training.
```

The output is a portable packet:

```text
examples + interventions + invariants + counterexamples + verifier hints +
compact executable frame
```

Manifesto fit:

- Genuine intelligence: acquiring an abstraction, not memorizing examples.
- Improvability: each failure can generate a new vaccine packet.
- Democratized development: communities can share inspectable lessons.
- Data efficiency: the premise is fewer, sharper examples.
- Inference efficiency: the frame can compile into small local logic.

Hardware path: CPU-first synthetic domains with small learners, then small local models only after the claim is sharp.

#### B: Self-Discovered Transformation Grammars

Core claim:

```text
The system induces valid transformations from observations, queries, and
admissibility feedback instead of receiving T and Phi from humans.
```

This directly attacks the B3-B4 gap, but as a standalone direction it risks becoming "typed edit enumeration with nicer words."

#### C: Pocket Proof Compiler

Core claim:

```text
Use an expensive model or human once, then compile repeated knowledge into a
tiny verified program that runs locally.
```

Strong on inference efficiency and democratized deployment, weaker on genuine intelligence unless the compiler discovers the frame.

#### D: Energy-Native Answer Economy

Core claim:

```text
Generation is wasteful. Cheap intelligence should score candidate worlds,
plans, or answers and let search/retrieval propose them.
```

Useful ingredient, but very vulnerable to "just a reranker."

#### E: Semantic Algebra

Core claim:

```text
Meaning has operations. If we discover the algebra, reasoning becomes exact
composition rather than statistical imitation.
```

Highest upside, but first experiments are easy to rig with supplied parsers or hand-coded symbolic semantics.

#### F: Failure-Patch Immune System

Core claim:

```text
Do not retrain after failure. Attach a small local proof-carrying patch that
fixes that failure class and composes with other patches.
```

Strong improvement story, but likely best as the update loop inside A.

#### G: Proxy-Function Divergence Science

Core claim:

```text
The central obstacle is knowing when optimization stops tracking the real
function.
```

Grounded in the kill history, but the public "so what" is weaker than a constructive cheap-intelligence direction.

#### Hostile Attack

The wide cast can be attacked as a rebrand of known fields:

- A: machine teaching, curriculum learning, active learning.
- B: metamorphic testing, grammar induction, type-driven edit enumeration.
- C: CEGIS, SyGuS, caching, LLM-written scripts.
- D: rerankers, verifiers, contrastive models.
- E: symbolic AI, algebraic semantics, embedding arithmetic.
- F: RAG corrections, rule patches, exception handling.
- G: Goodhart's law and train/test mismatch.

This is not a cheap shot. It is the default fate unless the first experiment gives the boring baselines equal information and lets them win if they can.

### Verdict

Leading candidates:

```text
A: FrameSeed / Intelligence Vaccines
B: Self-Discovered Transformation Grammars
E: Semantic Algebra
F: Failure-Patch Immune System
```

Most promising synthesis:

```text
A with B and F inside it: compact lessons that teach a cheap learner a new
transformation/verifier frame, and failed hidden cases become new local vaccine
packets.
```

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

```text
These are all slogans for old fields. You have not found a moonshot; you have
renamed curriculum learning, synthesis, and patching.
```

Answer:

```text
Correct unless the artifact creates or transmits a frame under fair baselines,
not merely solves a task using renamed old machinery.
```

## I220: Absorption Pre-Test

### Core Question

Which candidates collapse immediately under "that's trivial," and which survive the strongest boring baselines?

### Analysis

#### Steelman

The correct move is to kill early. Every candidate must face the simplest baseline that would absorb it.

#### A: FrameSeed / Intelligence Vaccines

Strongest trivial attack:

```text
This is just machine teaching, curriculum learning, active learning, or teaching
dimension with a better name.
```

Simplest absorbing baselines:

- Optimal active learner over the same hypothesis class, with the same learner, examples, labels, and query budget.
- CEGIS/program synthesis with the same DSL and counterexamples.
- RAG or few-shot prompt packet with nearest-neighbor examples.

Survival condition:

```text
The vaccine packet must install a frame that is not equivalent to active
example selection inside a supplied hypothesis class.
```

Concrete requirement: hold out the transformation/verifier/decomposition family. The learner starts with a generic representation language, not the correct task DSL. The packet must transfer to hidden schemas while active examples, RAG, and CEGIS fail or require much longer packets.

Status:

```text
SURVIVES, but only if framed as frame transfer rather than curriculum.
```

#### B: Self-Discovered Transformation Grammars

Strongest trivial attack:

```text
This is just enumerating type-derived edits: flip bools, substitute enums,
shift integers, rename IDs.
```

Simplest absorbing baselines:

- Typed edit enumerator: enumerate same-type substitutions and bounded shifts, filter by admissibility, score output covariance.
- Exhaustive bounded grammar enumeration up to length `L`.

Survival condition:

```text
The system must induce transformations not obvious from primitive type metadata,
or achieve hidden-schema transfer with much lower query cost and no smuggling.
```

Status:

```text
SURVIVES NARROWLY as a component of A, but weak as the standalone moonshot.
```

#### C: Pocket Proof Compiler

Strongest trivial attack:

```text
This is just program synthesis plus caching.
```

Simplest absorbing baselines:

- CEGIS/SyGuS enumerator plus property-based tests and a cache.
- LLM writes a script; tests verify it.

Survival condition would require the compiler to discover the useful frame, not only implement specified behavior.

Status:

```text
KILL AS STANDALONE. Keep as an execution layer for A or F.
```

#### D: Energy-Native Answer Economy

Strongest trivial attack:

```text
This is just a reranker, verifier, classifier, or contrastive model.
```

Simplest absorbing baselines:

- Logistic regression or small transformer reranker over candidate-answer pairs.
- Existing multiple-choice scoring with a pretrained model.

Status:

```text
KILL AS STANDALONE. Keep as a scoring head inside another direction.
```

#### E: Semantic Algebra

Strongest trivial attack:

```text
This is either hand-coded symbolic semantics or embedding arithmetic dressed up
as algebra.
```

Simplest absorbing baselines:

- Parser plus hand-coded denotation rules in the synthetic world.
- Linear probes, vector arithmetic, or a small seq2seq model on the same data.
- Semigroup/group enumeration with the same query access.

Survival condition:

```text
The algebra must be discovered from behavior and transfer to unseen composition
laws, not supplied by the benchmark or parser.
```

Status:

```text
SURVIVES AS HIGH-UPSIDER, but first experiment is hard to make non-rigged.
```

#### F: Failure-Patch Immune System

Strongest trivial attack:

```text
This is just RAG, rule patches, exception handling, or fine-tuning on failures.
```

Simplest absorbing baselines:

- Failure cache: store failed inputs, retrieve nearest failed case, override by lookup or prompt rule.
- Counterexample-guided program patching with a supplied patch DSL.

Survival condition:

```text
The patch must generalize to a hidden failure family while staying local,
composable, and shorter than enumerating examples.
```

Status:

```text
SURVIVES if merged with A; KILL as standalone.
```

#### G: Proxy-Function Divergence Science

Strongest trivial attack:

```text
This is Goodhart's law plus shortcut datasets. Everyone knows proxy metrics can
fail.
```

Simplest absorbing baseline: construct synthetic shortcut datasets and show proxy divergence with ordinary models.

Status:

```text
KILL AS NEXT MOONSHOT. Keep as an audit criterion.
```

### Verdict

Killed as standalone directions:

```text
C: Pocket Proof Compiler
D: Energy-Native Answer Economy
G: Proxy-Function Divergence Science
F: Failure-Patch Immune System as a standalone
```

Survivors:

```text
A: FrameSeed / Intelligence Vaccines
B: Self-Discovered Transformation Grammars
E: Semantic Algebra
F: Failure-Patch Immune System only as part of A
```

The strongest synthesis is:

```text
FrameSeed with transformation discovery and patchability inside it.
```

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

```text
The surviving direction is still just active learning plus CEGIS plus a patch
library. You have not escaped absorption; you only combined absorbed parts.
```

Answer:

```text
Possibly. Therefore the first experiment must let this attack win quickly. If
active learning, CEGIS, or failure-cache baselines match the vaccine under equal
information and packet-length budgets, the direction dies.
```

Headline:

```text
Tiny AI Gets Smart From Five Perfect Lessons Instead Of The Whole Internet
```

```text
If true, poor communities do not need billion-dollar training runs. They need
portable lesson packets that install useful frames into cheap local agents.
```

Isn't that obvious?

```text
People already know good teaching matters.
```

Counter:

```text
The non-obvious claim is not "teaching helps." The claim is that the right
lesson can transmit the hidden frame that makes a capability cheap, and that
the packet can be measured, shared, audited, and compiled.
```

Who cares?

```text
Anyone who cannot afford frontier inference, cloud fine-tuning, or massive data
collection. This is the manifesto in a headline.
```

Narrative verdict:

```text
STRONG.
```

```text
AI Learns What Counts As A Meaningful Change
```

So what?

```text
If a system can discover transformations, it can test itself, repair itself,
and avoid proxy traps.
```

Isn't that obvious?

```text
Testing tools already mutate inputs.
```

Counter:

```text
Mutation is not the claim. Discovering the admissible, meaning-preserving, and

```

Who cares?

```text
Researchers care. The public story is weaker unless tied to cheaper learning.
```

Narrative verdict:

```text
MEDIUM as standalone; STRONG as the engine inside FrameSeed.
```

#### E: Semantic Algebra

Headline:

```text
The Calculator For Meaning
```

So what?

```text
If meaning can be computed algebraically, inference becomes exact composition
instead of expensive sampling.
```

Isn't that obvious?

```text
Symbolic AI already tried this.
```

Counter:

```text
The new claim would have to be discovered algebra from behavior, not hand-coded
```

Who cares?

```text
Everyone would care if it worked. The risk is that the first demo looks like a

```

Narrative verdict:

```text
HIGH UPSIDE, LOW NEAR-TERM TRUST.
```

Headline:

```text
AI That Gets Vaccinated After Every Mistake
```

So what?

```text
Cheap systems could improve through local patches instead of retraining.
```

Isn't that obvious?

```text
Software already has patches. RAG already stores corrections.
```

Counter:

```text
The non-obvious version requires patches that generalize to hidden failure
```

Who cares?

```text
Every small deployer cares if it lets them maintain an AI without renting cloud
```

Narrative verdict:

```text
STRONG, but it should be the improvement loop inside FrameSeed.
```

The only story that survives both "that's trivial" and "who cares" is not a generic frame-discovery story. It is a teaching story:

```text
Instead of training a giant model on the whole internet, discover the tiny
lesson that teaches a cheap model the right way to think.
```

That story is public-readable, directly tied to democratization, and sharp enough to precommit.

### Verdict

The best public direction is:

```text
Intelligence Vaccines: compact frame-bearing lessons for cheap learners.
```

The phrase is only legitimate if the artifact has:

- a compact lesson packet;
- a transmitted frame;
- hidden-family transfer;
- a local verifier;
- an absorption ledger;
- a patch/update path after failure.

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

```text
"Intelligence vaccine" sounds like hype. You are promising five examples will
replace pretraining. That is obviously false.
```

Answer:

```text
The honest first claim is narrower: in controlled domains, can a compact
frame-bearing lesson beat much larger data, active-learning, RAG, and synthesis
baselines at transmitting a new abstraction to a weak learner? If not, kill it.
```

## I222: Cross-Pollination With PCCP-H

### Core Question

Can the next direction use PCCP-H's negative result instead of merely moving on from it?

### Analysis

#### Steelman

FrameSeed can use the best parts of PCCP-H without inheriting the dead parts.

Dead part:

```text
Mine over supplied transformations and call it intelligence.
```

Useful parts:

```text
absorption ladder
smuggling ledger
hidden-family transfer
equal-information baselines
function-vs-reconstruction theorem
nuisance-entropy gap
precommit verdict tokens
```

#### A New Teaching Ladder

The ladder should shift from "what clause did the miner find?" to "what frame did the lesson transmit?"

```text
T0: Rote examples
    The lesson is just a lookup table or prompt examples.

T1: Active curriculum
    The lesson is a good sequence of labeled examples inside a supplied
    hypothesis class.

T2: Counterexample-guided patch
    The lesson repairs a visible failure family using a supplied patch grammar.

T3: Frame-bearing lesson
    The lesson transmits an invariant, transformation, verifier, or
    decomposition not directly supplied to the learner.

T4: Self-generating frame teacher
    The teacher discovers which frame lesson to construct under a strict query
    and packet-length budget.

T5: Open-world frame formation
    Not claimed.
```

The first target should be T3, with T4 as the aspirational next step.

Precommit tokens:

```text
FRAMESEED_T3_SIGNAL
FRAMESEED_T3_ABSORBED_BY_ACTIVE_LEARNING
FRAMESEED_T3_ABSORBED_BY_CEGIS
FRAMESEED_T3_ABSORBED_BY_RAG
FRAMESEED_T3_VOID_SMUGGLED_FRAME
FRAMESEED_T3_NEGATIVE
```

#### Use Of Observational Equivalence

PCCP-H's theorem says observation-only data can be insufficient for the interventional target. FrameSeed turns that into a lesson-design rule:

```text
A valid vaccine cannot be only observational examples if hidden families differ
under intervention. It must include or induce the contrastive intervention that
identifies the frame.
```

First toy world:

```text
Two observationally equivalent rules produce the same public examples but
diverge under a targeted intervention. The vaccine packet must include the
minimal intervention that teaches the cheap learner which world it is in.
```

If the lesson does not include or induce the decisive intervention, it is surface fitting.

#### Use Of The Nuisance-Entropy Gap

PCCP-H says reconstruction pays for nuisance bits while functional programs can ignore them. FrameSeed turns this into a packet-length objective:

```text
The winning packet is not the one that reconstructs the world. It is the
shortest packet that teaches the functional frame and ignores nuisance entropy.
```

Measurement:

```text
As nuisance entropy m grows, active example packets and RAG packets should grow.
A true frame vaccine should stay approximately constant or grow much slower.
```

#### Combining Frame Discovery With Something Else

Frame discovery alone is too easy to overclaim. FrameSeed combines:

```text
frame discovery + teaching dimension + proof-carrying verifier + patchability
```

The unit of progress is not:

```text
The teacher found a transformation.
```

It is:

```text
The teacher found the compact lesson that makes a weaker learner use the right
transformation on unseen cases.
```

That is harder to fake because the frame must survive transfer through a constrained learner.

#### Hostile Attack

This may still collapse.

- If the learner's representation language already contains the right primitive, the vaccine only points to it.
- If the teacher is given the domain generator, it can solve the task and emit hand-picked examples. That is machine teaching.
- If the packet contains human-readable rules, the frame may be smuggled in the language prior.
- If hidden-family transfer is too close to public data, RAG will match it.

The defense is not rhetoric. The defense is to make these attacks first-class baselines.

### Verdict

```text
FrameSeed is the best way to reuse PCCP-H: keep the absorption discipline,
change the object from "discovered clause" to "transmitted frame."
```

PCCP-H becomes the immune system. Every vaccine claim must say what frame was supplied, what frame was taught, what baseline got equal information, and whether the transfer was absorbed.

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

```text
This is PCCP-H with extra packaging. You still need a human to design the world,
the interventions, the learner, and the verifier. The "vaccine" is just a
smaller supplied frame.
```

Answer:

```text
For the first experiment, yes: the domain is designed. The nontrivial claim is
whether the frame-bearing packet stays compact under nuisance growth and
transfers through a weaker learner where rote examples, active learning, RAG,
and CEGIS do not. If that fails, the direction dies before it becomes lore.
```

## I223: Feasibility Reality Check

### Core Question

What are the first decisive CPU-first experiments for the top candidates, and what verdict tokens should be precommitted?

### Analysis

#### Top Candidate 1: FrameSeed / Intelligence Vaccines

First experiment:

```text
FRAMESEED-0: Minimal Frame Vaccine Under Nuisance Growth
```

World:

```text
Inputs contain causal bits C, spurious bits S, nuisance bits N_1...N_m, and
ambiguous aliases that make two candidate frames observationally equivalent on
public examples.

The hidden-family target depends on the causal frame under intervention, not on
surface reconstruction.
```

Learners:

```text
L0: rote example learner / nearest-neighbor packet
L1: active learner over supplied feature hypotheses
L2: CEGIS/program learner with supplied DSL
L3: weak generic learner that can ingest examples, counterexamples, invariant
    clauses, transformation hints, and compact programs
```

Teacher packet:

```text
examples + targeted intervention examples + counterexamples + invariant hints +
local verifier clause + optional compact program
```

Success:

```text
FRAMESEED_T3_SIGNAL if:
  - L3 reaches hidden-family accuracy >= 0.95;
  - packet length grows sublinearly or stays nearly constant as nuisance m grows;
  - active learner, RAG packet, and CEGIS baselines fail the same accuracy under
    matched packet/query budget, or require at least 4x longer budget;
  - removing the intervention/counterexample/invariant component breaks transfer;
  - role permutation and name randomization do not change the verdict.
```

Failure:

```text
FRAMESEED_T3_ABSORBED_BY_ACTIVE_LEARNING if active learner matches.
FRAMESEED_T3_ABSORBED_BY_CEGIS if CEGIS matches.
FRAMESEED_T3_ABSORBED_BY_RAG if example retrieval matches.
FRAMESEED_T3_VOID_SMUGGLED_FRAME if the packet or learner contains the answer
as a named primitive.
FRAMESEED_T3_NEGATIVE if nobody transfers.
```

Time:

```text
1-2 days to design and implement cleanly.
Minutes per CPU run if kept finite and synthetic.
```

Precommit verdict token:

```text
FRAMESEED_T3_SIGNAL_OR_ABSORBED
```

#### Top Candidate 2: Self-Discovered Transformation Grammars

First experiment:

```text
B4-LITE: Ambiguous Typed Transformation Induction
```

World:

```text
Records contain ambiguous encodings:
  - integer-as-ID vs integer-as-count;
  - enum-as-mode vs enum-as-label;
  - timestamp-as-order vs timestamp-as-duration;
  - entity references requiring consistent renaming.
```

Task:

```text
Induce candidate transformations and output relations that transfer to hidden
schemas with changed names, arities, and nuisance fields.
```

Baselines:

```text
Typed edit enumerator.
Exhaustive bounded grammar enumerator.
Random transformation proposal with filtering.
Optional LLM/tool baseline later.
```

Success:

```text
B4_LITE_SIGNAL if the system discovers useful transformations not generated by
the typed edit enumerator under the same priors, or reaches the same hidden
transfer with a large query reduction that survives role permutation.
```

Failure:

```text
B4_LITE_ABSORBED if typed or exhaustive enumeration matches.
B4_LITE_VOID_TYPE_SMUGGLE if field types encode the intended transformations.
```

Time:

```text
1-3 days depending on how honest the ambiguous schema generator is.
CPU minutes per run.
```

Precommit verdict token:

```text
B4_LITE_SIGNAL_OR_ABSORBED
```

#### Top Candidate 3: Semantic Algebra

First experiment:

```text
SEMALG-0: Discovered Composition Tables From Behavioral Queries
```

World:

```text
Tiny command language with hidden operations over objects, relations, and
states. Surface words are permuted. Some operations compose, commute, invert,
or annihilate. The learner can query behavior but is not given parse semantics.
```

Task:

```text
Infer an algebra of operations that predicts unseen compositions and transfers
to renamed worlds.
```

Baselines:

```text
Hand-coded parser with grammar.
Seq2seq model trained on examples.
Table lookup over observed compositions.
Group/semigroup enumeration with same query access.
```

Success:

```text
SEMALG_DISCOVERY_SIGNAL if the discovered algebra predicts long unseen
compositions with much less data than seq2seq/table baselines and is not
matched by semigroup enumeration under equal information.
```

Failure:

```text
SEMALG_ABSORBED_BY_ENUMERATION if algebra enumeration matches.
SEMALG_VOID_PARSER_SMUGGLE if the grammar is supplied.
SEMALG_NEGATIVE if no transfer.
```

Time:

```text
2-5 days to avoid a rigged parser toy.
CPU minutes per small world.
```

Precommit verdict token:

```text
SEMALG_SIGNAL_OR_ABSORBED
```

#### Hostile Feasibility Check

All three are synthetic. That is acceptable only if the synthetic result is treated as an absorption filter, not as a victory.

The practical order should be:

```text
1. FRAMESEED-0, because it has the strongest manifesto and narrative fit.
2. B4-LITE, if FrameSeed needs a transformation-discovery submodule.
3. SEMALG-0 only after the parser-smuggling risk is controlled.
```

### Verdict

```text
The first decisive test should be FRAMESEED-0.
```

Reason:

```text
It is the best intersection of manifesto alignment, narrative strength,
PCCP-H lessons, feasibility, and killability.
```

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

```text
Your first experiment is synthetic again. Another Boolean toy will not convince
anyone that cheap general intelligence is possible.
```

Answer:
