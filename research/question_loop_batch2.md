## Iteration 8: Existence Attack - Maybe There Is No Compact Geometry

### Current Strongest Position

GISBE says a trained teacher contains a compact causal relational geometry. The teacher's private coordinates are disposable, but its task-relevant distinctions, transformations, and margin effects may be public. If those public relational objects admit a compact basis, a byte-native student can inherit semantic addressability without ordinary KD.

### Steelman

This is the cleanest remaining interpretation of "Intelligence = Geometry."

The failed repo history points away from logits, raw hidden states, byte marginals, and naive width. What remains is not "copy the teacher's outputs" but "find the coordinate system that makes meaning addressable." A teacher may have learned a reusable semantic geometry: contexts cluster by action affordances, candidate continuations live in a margin landscape, transformations preserve or flip decisions in structured ways, and many decisions share hidden causal variables.

The evidence that makes this plausible:

- language has compositional structure;
- transformers reuse mechanisms across contexts;
- MLP memories and attention heads suggest repeated computational motifs;
- candidate margins often depend on shared semantic variables like agents, objects, affordances, causality, and temporal order;
- CBD's 138M result implies a small model can store more capability than from-scratch training reveals, if the right coordinate system is inherited.

GISBE also fits the codec result. A byte-to-token embedding codec is already a coordinate chart from byte space into the teacher's lexical coordinate system. It is not yet semantic geometry, but it is the first actual bridge in the repo that can map byte sequences into a teacher-adjacent latent alphabet.

### Attack

The strongest objection is existential: the compact object may not exist.

Commonsense in an LLM may be less like a geometry and more like a giant compressed associative memory. The model may know that roofs are repaired, bars are lifted, canoes are paddled, and basketballs are dribbled because these patterns are distributed across billions of training examples and millions of weakly interacting slots. There may be local low-rank structure everywhere but no global low-rank atlas that preserves the distinctions HellaSwag needs.

This would explain every local positive signal that later died:

- byte KD improved surface modeling because byte statistics are compact;
- toy binding had clean latent variables because the task was synthetically factorized;
- hidden alignment found marginal geometry because teacher activations do have statistics;
- ranking losses sometimes created order parameters but not stable mechanisms;
- codec Phase 1 learned token identity because lexical mapping is compact;
- Wide7 learned bytes faster because byte prediction is low-level and repetitive.

None of that proves compact HellaSwag-level semantic geometry. The teacher's real commonsense may be a high-entropy warehouse, not a periodic table.

The most dangerous version: GISBE might pass beautiful Track A tests on toy or microdomain transformations while failing real HellaSwag because real commonsense is a long tail of activity-specific memories. "Geometry exists" for grammar, token identity, and toy binding; "geometry exists" for embodied social/physical continuation may be false at the compression level needed.

### What Survived

The existence claim survives only if it becomes narrower and measurable:

There may be compact semantic address geometry for a subset of continuation judgment, not necessarily a whole-model world model.

The first GISBE artifact should not claim to contain all facts. It should claim to provide an address system that makes facts easier to retrieve, acquire, or score. The geometry might be a routing scaffold, not the knowledge itself.

Track A must therefore split:

1. **Addressability geometry:** Do contexts/candidates map into a compact space where semantic neighborhoods and transformation laws are stable?
2. **Knowledge payload:** Does that space already contain enough facts to answer benchmarks?

If Track A only proves addressability, the step-zero claim must be limited to "born with instincts" or "born with a semantic compass," not "born with all knowledge."

### What Died

Dead or demoted:

- "The teacher contains one compact basis for general intelligence."
- "A low-rank semantic atlas will directly encode HellaSwag facts."
- "Track A success on margin geometry automatically implies Track B benchmark lift."
- Any claim that token identity retrieval is already semantic structure.

### New Leading Direction

GISBE becomes a two-layer hypothesis:

```text
Layer 1: Semantic address geometry
  The compact object maps byte strings into coordinates where meanings,
  affordances, and candidate relations are easier to locate.

Layer 2: Knowledge payload
  Facts and skills may still require inherited weights, retrieval,
  curriculum, or later training.
```

This is less grand but harder to fake. The first target is not "install all of Qwen in Sutra." The first target is "install the coordinate system that lets Sutra find meaning immediately and learn facts much faster."

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

1. "That's obvious" dismissal: Of course models have some semantic structure. Embeddings have done that for years.
2. "That's trivial" dismissal: If the compact object is only an embedding space, this is just a tokenizer/embedding preprocessor.
3. What the result needs to BE for unkillable narrative: A newborn byte model with a compiled geometry module must make correct semantic discriminations on examples never used to extract the module, while lexical, shuffled, and random-coordinate controls fail.

### Gossip-Magazine Headline

A baby AI is born with a sense of meaning, not a library of memorized answers.

### Next Iteration Starting Position

Attack the assumption that even an address geometry can cross from a token transformer into a byte-patch Sutra body.

## Iteration 9: Cross-Architecture Attack - The Codec Is a Gauge Map, But Maybe Only a Lexical One

### Current Strongest Position

After the existence attack, GISBE no longer claims to extract a complete world model. It claims to extract a semantic address geometry. The obvious bridge is the existing semantic codec: bytes go in, teacher token embeddings come out. If GISBE needs a gauge-fixing interface, the codec is not optional infrastructure. It is the current gauge chart.

### Steelman

This is the first place where GISBE becomes concrete.

The core cross-architecture problem is that Qwen and Sutra do not share a body. Qwen is a token transformer with a private residual stream. Sutra is byte-patch/global with different lag, patching, width, depth, and output mechanics. Direct hidden-state alignment already failed. Raw weight transplant is gauge nonsense. A compiler needs an interface where both sides can speak.

The codec can serve that role:

```text
bytes -> codec hidden -> teacher embedding chart -> semantic basis
```

Instead of mapping Qwen hidden states directly into Sutra hidden states, GISBE can first map byte strings into the teacher's lexical chart. Then extraction and compilation happen over relational objects in that chart:

- context/candidate embeddings;
- teacher margin gradients projected into the chart;
- transformation laws over paraphrases and counterfactuals;
- low-rank candidate energy operators;
- feature/slot effects validated against teacher margins.

This gives the compile step a real shape.

### Attack

The codec may be the wrong gauge map.

The Phase 1 result says the codec can retrieve a teacher token embedding from the raw bytes of the token about 61.6% of the time. That is impressive, but it is mostly a byte-to-token coordinate chart. It may be equivalent to building a soft tokenizer. A tokenizer is useful, but it is not a semantic transplant.

The mismatch is severe:

- Phase 1 supervised token ends, while Phase 2 consumes every 4-byte patch boundary.
- Only roughly 20% of Phase 2 patch states coincide with Phase 1 supervised boundaries.
- The codec sees the token's own bytes, so token identity can dominate.
- Fixed shuffled controls remained far above chance, so local frequency/geometric artifacts are real.
- The codec has not proven paraphrase invariance, candidate-margin preservation, or causal relation to HellaSwag decisions.

If this codec becomes the GISBE gauge map, GISBE may inherit the exact old failure mode: a clean lexical bridge that still does not deliver semantic addressability. The compiler would become "soft tokenizer plus ranking head," and the adversary will call it byte-to-token KD with better PR.

The deeper cross-architecture problem remains: even if Qwen's semantic basis is represented in its embedding chart, Sutra's reasoner may not execute Qwen's transition operators. A chart maps coordinates. It does not guarantee the new body can run the dynamics.

### What Survived

The codec survives as the required gauge interface, but only after being demoted and hardened.

It is not "the semantic codec." It is:

```text
C: byte-to-teacher-token gauge chart
```

GISBE must then add the missing semantic layer:

```text
B: causal relational basis in the codec chart
T: transition operators over basis states
E: candidate energy functional
I: insertion maps into Sutra modules
M: controls manifest
```

The compile step must be explicit:

1. **Gauge chart:** Use the codec to map byte spans and candidate continuations into teacher-token embedding coordinates. Dense patch-boundary supervision or a patch-boundary control is mandatory.
2. **Teacher measurement:** Measure teacher candidate margins and selected activation/Jacobian effects, then project all effects into the codec chart.
3. **Basis extraction:** Solve a low-rank relational basis `B_k` by linear algebra over Gram matrices, margin-effect matrices, and transformation-consistency constraints.
4. **Executable module:** Emit a frozen candidate scorer:

   ```text
   phi(x) = pool(B_k^T C(x))
   phi(y) = pool(B_k^T C(y))
   score_0(x,y) = phi(x)^T M phi(y) + a^T [phi(x), phi(y), phi(x)*phi(y)]
   ```

   `M` and `a` must come from closed-form regression or spectral decomposition over teacher relational measurements, not student gradient training.

5. **Sutra insertion:** Initialize low-rank hooks or a judgment head from `B_k, T, M`, then test before ordinary CE/KD can explain the lift.

This is still not pure zero optimization. It is a compiled birth package. The evidence claim is not "no gradients occurred anywhere." The evidence claim is "the newborn Sutra module behaves intelligently immediately after assembly, before training the student."

### What Died

Dead:

- Treating the existing codec as semantic proof.
- Compiling directly from Qwen hidden coordinates into Sutra hidden coordinates.
- Any GISBE plan without a concrete executable output.
- Any compile step that secretly trains Sutra against teacher examples and calls the result geometry.

### New Leading Direction

GISBE must become **codec-gauge Brainseed**:

```text
teacher checkpoint + codec gauge chart -> compact birth package
birth package -> frozen zero-step judgment module + optional Sutra initialization
```

The codec is the coordinate interface. The semantic basis is the missing content. The zero-step judgment module is the first honest compile target because it can be evaluated immediately without pretending the whole autoregressive model has been solved.

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

1. "That's obvious" dismissal: This is a soft tokenizer plus an energy head.
2. "That's trivial" dismissal: The codec just learns token identity; all real knowledge still comes from teacher margins.
3. What the result needs to BE for unkillable narrative: The codec-only scorer must fail, while the causal relational basis on top of the codec succeeds on held-out semantic transformations and real benchmark slices.

### Gossip-Magazine Headline

Scientists teach a newborn byte-AI the language spoken inside a grown AI's brain.

### Next Iteration Starting Position

Attack whether the birth package can survive 5:1 compression into a 121M model without collapsing into a tiny benchmark adapter.

## Iteration 10: Scale Attack - 5:1 Compression May Kill the Magic

### Current Strongest Position

GISBE has been made concrete as a codec-gauge Brainseed: use the codec as the byte-to-teacher gauge chart, extract a compact causal relational basis, and compile it into a frozen zero-step judgment module plus optional Sutra initialization.

### Steelman

This answers the supervisor's compile objection better than Batch 1 did. The student is not merely trained to match teacher hidden states. The extractor produces an executable module:

```text
C = codec gauge chart
B = relational basis
T = transition laws
E = energy functional
I = insertion maps
```

The first proof is a birth test: before Sutra receives ordinary training updates, the compiled module scores contexts and candidates better than random, lexical, codec-only, shuffled-margin, and rotated-basis controls. This is the "born knowing" moment. It does not require pretending the extraction process used no computation. It requires that the student wakes up with capability it did not learn through its own training history.

### Attack

The 5pp HellaSwag gate may be fantasy at 121M.

The repo already proved real S0 hidden states do not contain HellaSwag knowledge. Wide7 improved byte modeling dramatically but stayed at chance-like reasoning. CBD's 42.65% at 138M likely comes from inherited same-family coordinates and compressed pretrained knowledge, not from a tiny model discovering commonsense.

If GISBE extracts addressability but not facts, step-zero HellaSwag lift may be small. HellaSwag asks about thousands of activity-specific physical/social priors. A compact basis can say "actions have affordances" but may not know which affordances belong to roofing, high jumping, canoeing, plumbing, or restaurant behavior. The missing payload is not geometry alone.

This creates a narrative danger. If the first public number is "+1.2pp HellaSwag at step zero," the world shrugs. If the internal story is "but the basis predicts teacher margins 12% better than PCA," nobody cares. The manifesto says stop-scrolling results or failure.

Worse, if the birth package includes a frozen scorer outside the 121M parameter budget, critics can say the model did not get smarter; it got a benchmark-specific prosthetic. If the module is large, it violates inference efficiency. If it is tiny, it may lack facts.

### What Survived

The scale attack forces a better success metric.

The viral claim is not "zero training purity." It is:

```text
The model was smart the moment it was created, before its own training could explain it.
```

That can be measured two ways:

1. **Birth advantage:** The compiled module has immediate held-out capability at step zero.
2. **Learning acceleration:** The same module makes Sutra learn semantic tasks far faster than a matched baseline.

For 121M, the honest primary gate should shift from ">=5pp HellaSwag immediately" to:

- Step-zero microdomain and transformation tests must show large specific lift, e.g. +20pp over codec-only and shuffled controls.
- Step-zero real benchmark slices must move in the right direction and beat controls, but full HellaSwag +5pp is a stretch goal.
- After a small fixed training budget, the Brainseed model must show a large data-efficiency multiplier, e.g. reach in 5K-10K steps what the baseline cannot reach in 50K-100K, or produce >=3pp cross-task aggregate lift at matched tokens.
- The artifact must stay small enough that it is a birth package, not a hidden teacher.

This is not lowering the ambition. It is choosing a gate the evidence can honestly support.

### What Died

Dead:

- "Geometry alone must immediately add 5pp HellaSwag or GISBE is dead."
- "No gradients anywhere" as the core moral line.
- Step-zero full benchmark victory as the first required proof.
- A frozen benchmark adapter too large or too task-specific to be considered a small model module.

### New Leading Direction

GISBE's first public proof should be a **born-knowing curve**, not a single final benchmark number:

```text
x-axis: student training tokens after birth
y-axis: held-out semantic judgment accuracy
lines: random init, codec-only, Brainseed, shuffled Brainseed, rotated Brainseed
```

The shocking result is a vertical jump at token zero plus a steeper slope afterward. The model starts ahead and learns faster. If the Brainseed line begins above the baseline before any student training and stays ahead under matched compute, the story is real.

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

1. "That's obvious" dismissal: Pretrained modules make models better at step zero. That is not new.
2. "That's trivial" dismissal: You attached a small classifier or retrieval table.
3. What the result needs to BE for unkillable narrative: The birth package must be automatically extracted from a teacher checkpoint, compact, teacher-free at inference, cross-domain, and killed by shuffled/rotated/lexical controls.

### Gossip-Magazine Headline

This AI did not become smart while training. It woke up with instincts.

### Next Iteration Starting Position

Attack whether the extraction and validation can actually run on a single 5090, or whether causal geometry becomes a cluster-scale interpretability project.

## Iteration 11: Feasibility Attack - Causal Patching Can Eat the Whole Laptop

### Current Strongest Position

GISBE is now a birth-package program: use the codec as gauge chart, extract a causal relational basis, compile a zero-step judgment module, and measure birth advantage plus learning acceleration. Full HellaSwag step-zero lift is a stretch, not the primary gate.

### Steelman

This can be made laptop-shaped if the extractor is carefully staged.

The Work Loop already argued that activation caches, CCA, randomized SVD, low-rank regression, and Jacobian sketches are cheap relative to training. Qwen3-0.6B is not frontier-scale. A single RTX 5090 can run forward passes and limited backward measurements. The codec chart reduces the dimensionality and avoids trying to align every teacher hidden state directly to Sutra.

A feasible extraction ladder:

```text
Tier 0: codec-only lexical chart
  No causal patching. Test whether C(x), C(y) already support semantic scoring.

Tier 1: sketched margin geometry
  For selected contexts/candidates, compute teacher candidate margins and
  low-rank gradients/Jacobian sketches with respect to selected layers or
  codec-chart variables.

Tier 2: targeted causal validation
  Patch or ablate only the top-k discovered directions/features on a small
  held-out set. Use this to validate causality, not discover everything.

Tier 3: compiled birth test
  Freeze the artifact, run Sutra/Brainseed against held-out tasks and controls.
```

The key is not to train giant SAEs or perform exhaustive intervention maps. Use causal patching as an adversarial audit of the extracted directions.

### Attack

This may still blow up.

Candidate-margin causal geometry is expensive because every useful measurement depends on context, candidate set, layer, feature direction, and transformation family. If you have 10,000 contexts, 4 candidates, 28 teacher layers, 1,024 hidden dimensions, and multiple interventions, the naive grid is dead. Even if each forward pass is cheap, the combinatorics are not.

Cheap sketches introduce their own failure mode. If Tier 1 only uses gradients or covariance, it may rediscover high-variance lexical directions rather than causal semantics. If Tier 2 patches only top directions, it may validate a biased shortlist. If the extractor is too small, a failed result may be a false negative; if it is big enough, it violates the single-laptop thesis.

SAEs are especially dangerous here. Training a sparse feature dictionary on Qwen activations can become its own research project, with hidden hyperparameters, reconstruction losses, feature splitting, and interpretability theater. A 5090 can run toy SAEs, but not a whole-world causal atlas with confidence.

The story also suffers if extraction takes days and dozens of manual choices. "A laptop read a brain in two hours" is shocking. "We tuned feature dictionaries for a week" is not.

### What Survived

GISBE survives only as a **sketched causal compiler**, not a full mechanistic interpretability atlas.

Strict feasibility constraints:

- Use 1k-5k extraction contexts for first real run, not 50k+.
- Use selected teacher layers, not all layers.
- Use randomized sketches and low-rank margin matrices before any feature learning.
- Validate the top 64-512 directions with targeted patching.
- Avoid SAE training in the first proof unless every simpler basis fails and the SAE has matched shuffled controls.
- Cache only what the birth package needs.
- Report extraction wall-clock time and artifact size as first-class metrics.

The extraction should produce a small file, not a new checkpoint:

```text
brainseed_v0/
  codec_manifest.json
  basis_B.fp16
  energy_M.fp16
  transition_T.fp16
  insertion_I.fp16
  controls_manifest.json
  extraction_report.md
```

The adversary should be able to inspect the artifact and see that it is not a hidden teacher model.

### What Died

Dead:

- Whole-model causal patching as the first GISBE experiment.
- Training large SAEs as required evidence.
- Any plan whose compute or manual choices exceed the 5090 story.
- Any "geometry" artifact too large to publish and audit.

### New Leading Direction

GISBE becomes:

```text
Sketched Brainseed Extraction

Teacher + codec chart + 1k-5k contexts
  -> low-rank relational basis
  -> targeted causal audit
  -> compact birth package
  -> immediate candidate judgment test
```

This is falsifiable. If the small sketch cannot beat controls, stop. Do not scale the patching ritual until the cheap version shows a real specific signal.

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

1. "That's obvious" dismissal: This is CCA/PCA/Jacobian compression with controls.
2. "That's trivial" dismissal: The impressive part is the teacher; the extractor is a measurement script.
3. What the result needs to BE for unkillable narrative: The full extraction must be cheap, automatic, compact, and produce immediate held-out behavior, with public controls that make the fake versions fail.

### Gossip-Magazine Headline

A laptop scans a giant AI for two hours and prints a seed for a smaller mind.

### Next Iteration Starting Position

Attack the story itself. Even if the method is technically defensible, "GISBE" may be too abstract to win attention.

## Iteration 12: Narrative Attack - GISBE Is Not the Public Story

### Current Strongest Position

GISBE has become a sketched Brainseed extraction pipeline. Technically: codec gauge chart, low-rank relational basis, targeted causal audit, compact birth package, zero-step candidate judgment, and learning-acceleration curves. The public-facing version is still unclear.

### Steelman

The lab name can stay GISBE. It is precise enough for internal research:

```text
Gauge-Invariant Semantic Basis Extraction
```

It signals the actual technical thesis:

- gauge dependence matters;
- hidden coordinates are not transferable;
- semantic relations are the target;
- a compact basis is the artifact;
- extraction and compilation are distinct.

For researchers, this is a serious frame. It avoids overclaiming. It connects to the failure history and to the Work Loop's gauge-transplant gauntlet.

### Attack

Normal people do not care about gauge invariance.

"Scientists decode the coordinate system for meaning" is clever but not shocking. It sounds like an embedding paper. It invites the "so what?" response. Worse, it hides the emotional punch of the real idea: a model can be created already capable because we extracted the shape of another model's learned mind.

The user's complaint was never just "do not use gradients." The complaint was "do not call ordinary distillation a moonshot." The viral moment is not purity. It is birth:

```text
The AI was smart the moment it was created.
```

That frame changes the evidence standard. A training curve that starts above baseline at token zero is legible to everyone. The model did not learn that capability through its own training because the training had not happened yet. It was born with it.

GISBE also underplays the laptop constraint. The normal-person story is not "a basis extraction algorithm." It is:

```text
A single laptop read a grown AI's brain scan and printed a newborn AI with instincts.
```

This is shocking if true. It also forces honesty: if the result is only a probe score or a hidden alignment metric, the headline collapses.

### What Survived

GISBE survives as the technical method, but the direction's public frame should be **Brainseed** or **Born-Knowing Sutra**.

The research artifact can define both:


## Iteration 13: Alternative Attack - Maybe Brainseed Is Still Too Incremental

### Current Strongest Position

The survivor is now GISBE as internal method and Brainseed as public artifact. The model receives a compact, teacher-free birth package that gives immediate semantic instincts and faster learning.

### Steelman

Brainseed has a rare combination:

- It addresses the actual byte-to-semantics bottleneck.
- It uses the existing codec as a gauge chart instead of ignoring repo evidence.
- It avoids direct hidden alignment.
- It produces a compact artifact instead of a dataset.
- It can be tested on one RTX 5090.
- It has a normal-person story.

It also keeps Sutra's identity. The model is still byte-native. The teacher is a specimen, not a runtime dependency. The product is not a smaller Qwen. It is a new body born with a seed extracted from another mind.

### Attack

There may be a better home-run frame that makes Brainseed look timid.

**Alternative 1: Byteified chain-init.** Take a pretrained token model, wrap it with byte I/O, then prune or distill to smaller Sutra. This is more likely to produce high HellaSwag quickly. It directly attacks the manifesto target of beating SmolLM2. The story is less pure, but the result may be more shocking because the benchmark number will be real.

**Alternative 2: Retrieval-born Sutra.** Do not store world knowledge in 121M weights. Give the model a compact open memory and train it to reason over retrieved context. This could produce immediate HellaSwag gains with far less compression pressure. The headline "tiny AI beats giants because it knows how to use a library" is less magical but more practical.

**Alternative 3: Active MoE.** Keep 121M active parameters but allow 300M-500M total parameters. Experts separate byte mechanics, semantic addressability, and world knowledge. This may satisfy inference efficiency better than dense 121M and avoid impossible compression.

**Alternative 4: Circuit genome benchmark.** Forget broad HellaSwag. Extract typed executable circuits for binding, comparison, negation, temporal order, and physical affordance. If a teacher-learned trick becomes a program that a small model can run, that is more paradigm-level than a fuzzy semantic basis.

**Alternative 5: Open lesson compiler.** Turn teacher disagreements into reusable lesson packets that the community can inspect and extend. This directly serves democratized development and improvability. It may be more manifesto-aligned than another hidden geometry artifact.

Brainseed could be a local optimum: technically sophisticated, narratively strong, but still not enough to beat Arjun. If it only produces a modest birth lift and a mild training speedup, it fails the Vision.

### What Survived

Brainseed survives only if it is positioned as the **first birth mechanism**, not the whole strategy.

The sharper direction is:

```text
Born-Knowing Sutra is the objective.
GISBE/Brainseed is the first candidate mechanism.
```

This distinction matters. If byteified chain-init produces a stronger born-knowing curve, it should win. If retrieval produces a more shocking small-model result, it should win. The manifesto values outcomes over mechanisms.

Brainseed's unique role is to test the most interesting hypothesis:

```text
Can a compact semantic coordinate system be extracted from a teacher and installed
cross-architecture before training?
```

If yes, it becomes the seed layer for chain-init, retrieval, MoE, and lesson compilers. If no, it dies cleanly and the project pivots to the stronger alternative.

### What Died

Dead:

- GISBE as sacred.
- 121M closed-weight purity as sacred.
- "No retrieval" as a hidden constraint.
- Refusing chain-init because it feels less philosophically pure.
- Treating a small technical lift as success if it does not serve the Vision.

### New Leading Direction

Run a **Born-Knowing Gauntlet** where Brainseed competes against alternatives:

```text
Contestants:
  random Sutra
  codec-only Sutra
  Brainseed Sutra
  shuffled Brainseed
  byteified-chain-init baseline if available
  retrieval-born baseline if available
  task-specific lesson compiler baseline

Metrics:
  step-zero held-out semantic judgment
  HellaSwag/PIQA/ARC movement after tiny fixed training
  artifact size
  teacher calls at inference
  extraction time
  data-efficiency multiplier
```

The direction is not "prove GISBE." The direction is "find the mechanism that makes a small AI born with useful intelligence on a laptop."

### NARRATIVE ATTACK

1. "That's obvious" dismissal: This is just pretraining by another name.
2. "That's trivial" dismissal: If retrieval or chain-init wins, the geometry story was unnecessary.
3. What the result needs to BE for unkillable narrative: Brainseed must beat codec-only and retrieval-lite controls on compactness, immediacy, and transfer, or concede to the stronger born-knowing mechanism.
