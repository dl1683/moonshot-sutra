## What Was Learned

1. Perfect hidden HFA is not a signal by itself. In both domains, L3 reached perfect hidden accuracy.
2. Boolean FRAMESEED-0 was absorbed because exact finite teaching/search also reached perfect hidden accuracy.
3. SHEETS-0 was absorbed because charged task bindings plus typed pipeline baselines solved the hidden tasks; binding-only HFA was 1.0 and packet-erasure drop was 0.0.
4. The current supplied-packet formulation collapses into known objects: teaching dimension, schema binding, PBE, typed CEGIS, data wrangling, nuisance oracle, and library learning.
5. The absorption ladder worked. The strongest current artifact is the methodology for killing or reframing attractive ideas cleanly.

## Current Position

Do not continue FrameSeed as the main moonshot direction in its current form. Another supplied-packet domain would need a new precommitted non-absorption claim before implementation; otherwise it is likely to repeat the same failure.

The only serious FrameSeed-adjacent reframe is to move from frame transmission to frame discovery:

```text
Self-discovered transformation grammars in typed practical domains.
```

That direction should be treated as a new moonshot candidate, not as a FrameSeed win. It must give typed CEGIS, library learning, schema binding, active goal disambiguation, PBE, and oracle baselines equal information and first refusal.

Acceptable alternate redirects:

- typed CEGIS/proof compilation as the honest substrate;
- MDL library learning as the honest substrate;
- active counterexample selection as the honest substrate;
- schema-binding science as the honest substrate.

## Methodology To Preserve

- Precommitted terminal tokens before implementation.
- Absorber-first design: strongest boring explanations become executable.
- Equal-information baseline parity.
- Hidden seed freeze and no post-hidden mutation under the same seed.
- Packet-erasure and ablation tests.
- Role/schema/name/unit/row permutation stability.
- Cost splits for frame, binding, program, parser substrate, human labor, and residual teaching bits.
- AFTD-style amortization for any claimed reusable frame.
- Strict claim ceilings.
- Negative results as primary research output when they remove a real assumption.
