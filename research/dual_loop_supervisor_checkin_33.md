## 1. Direction Recommendation

Q-Loop B41 recommends **World Grammar Discovery (WGD)**:

```
Can a cheap system discover the transformation and obligation geometry of a
new world well enough to act, repair, abstain, and transfer, under hostile
native absorbers and clean all-in accounting?
```

This directly attacks FrameSeed's cause of death: FrameSeed transmitted
supplied geometry. WGD asks whether the system can DISCOVER geometry.

### Narrative Check

**Gossip sentence**: "What if tiny AI can figure out the rules of a new
world on its own — without being taught?"

- Survives "isn't that obvious?": Not obvious — current systems either get
  the rules supplied (FrameSeed, prompting, fine-tuning) or brute-force
  search them (PBE, CEGIS). WGD asks for something between: discovery of
  reusable structure.
- Survives "that's trivial": The absorbers (PBE, CEGIS, library learning,
  active learning) are strong. If WGD survives them, it's genuinely novel.
- Narrative verdict: **ALIVE and stronger than FrameSeed's narrative.**

### Risk Assessment

Main risks:
1. PBE/CEGIS/library learning absorb WGD the same way they absorbed FrameSeed
2. "Discovery" is smuggled through the supplied substrate/operations
3. Toy domains don't generalize to real worlds

These are real but the FrameSeed immune system (absorption ladder, precommit
tokens, geometry-erasure, equal-information baselines) directly addresses them.
